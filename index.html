01 / About

Research driven by
experimental science.

Connecting fundamental fluid mechanics with practical challenges in energy, propulsion and the environment.

I am an experimental researcher with experience in combustion, multiphase flows, turbulent mixing and optical diagnostics. My work combines the development of laboratory-scale experimental facilities with time-resolved flow and flame measurements.

At IIT Madras, I contribute to the development of gas-generation and fuel-injection systems for high-speed propulsion research. My previous research includes spray combustion and droplet-cluster gasification at IIT Madras, and turbulence propagation in density-stratified fluids at the University of Melbourne.

I am interested in translating advances in experimental fluid mechanics and heat transfer into technologies for clean energy, efficient thermal systems and advanced propulsion.

“Building experimental capabilities to understand complex flows and develop more efficient energy systems.”

02 / Research

Research interests

Experimental investigations spanning reacting and non-reacting flows, transport phenomena and propulsion-relevant systems.

Reacting flows

Combustion and thermoacoustics

Flame dynamics, hydrodynamic and thermoacoustic interactions, stability, blow-off and control of combustion systems.

Multiphase flows

Sprays and droplet dynamics

Droplet clustering, atomization, collective gasification and spray combustion in practical fuel-injection configurations.

Turbulence

Turbulent mixing and stratified flows

Turbulence propagation, entrainment and mixing across density interfaces, with relevance to environmental and geophysical flows.

Propulsion

High-speed propulsion systems

Experimental development of gas generators, fuel-injection systems and combustor concepts for ramjet and scramjet applications.

Diagnostics

Laser diagnostics and imaging

Development and application of PIV, PLIF, ILIDS, shadowgraphy and high-speed imaging for quantitative flow measurements.

Thermal sciences

Energy and thermal systems

Applying fluid mechanics and heat transfer to efficient thermal management, clean fuels and next-generation energy technologies.
